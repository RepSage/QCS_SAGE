# QCS v5.1 — Time-of-day standardized cross-site panels + replicate spread band

> **MINOR** release (v5.0 → v5.1), developed on branch `improvements-v5.1`.
> Visualization-only changes: no QC test, threshold or flag behavior is altered.
> Note: the version bump resets saved QC criteria to the (unchanged) defaults on
> first launch, per the version-gated settings rule.

## Cross-site panels standardized by time of day (B6)

When sites are sampled on **different days**, what matters for comparison is the
**time of day**. The two cross-site panels no longer use elapsed-time or
absolute-date X axes:

- **"Parameter across sites" (Panel 2)** and **"HOBO Light multi-site"** now plot
  against **hours since midnight of each site's own first sampled day**: x = 15
  is 15:00 of day 1 for every site, day 2 continues at 24-48 h, and so on. Sites
  sampled on different dates **overlay by clock time** while the deployment
  sequence is preserved.
- Ticks are labeled with the **clock hour** (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, ...) repeating
  identically day after day, with a light dashed line at each **day boundary**
  (multiples of 24 h). Tick steps are clock-aligned (1/2/3/6/12/24 h).
- The **X-axis time window** follows the same rule on these panels: its **day
  offset plus clock time** apply to each site's own days (e.g. a window from
  day-1 06:00 to day-2 12:00 selects that range of every site's own deployment).
  With a single site this reduces exactly to the absolute window. Panel 1 and
  the per-site HOBO panels keep absolute dates (they show one site at a time).
- The HOBO multi-site fouling **cutoff markers** are drawn at the corresponding
  anchored hour (the cutoff date stays in the legend label).

## HOBO combined replicates — spread band

- The **HOBO temperature panel** now shades the **between-replicate
  disagreement** when the file carries the `Temperature spread (degC)` column
  (combined-replicates output): a band of total width = spread (max - min),
  centered on the plotted mean, labeled "Replicate spread (max - min)" in the
  legend. Single-replicate files are unchanged (the column is empty).
