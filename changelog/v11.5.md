> replicates sheet (spread column, below).

## The .hobo reader graduates (no longer misses or misreads)

Three structural discoveries, each validated against the archive's 200+
file/export pairs:

- **Logger event markers live INSIDE the sample stream** (temp bits all
  ones), and each un-skipped marker slid the alignment by one slot from
  there on — this was the entire family of files that decoded ~50–75%. The
  reader now walks the stream skipping isolated markers (they consume no
  time slot) and stops only where the continuation stops fitting the
  calibration.
- **The preamble ends with a fixed delimiter signature** (bits 4–13 set,
  low nibble clear — census over 185 export-proven files), and a sample can
  never look like it. Sample 0 is anchored 18 bits after the LAST delimiter
  match; the content scan remains only as fallback. This removed the
  one-slot misses at the head (a real first reading taken on a hot deck is
  no longer confused with a leftover preamble token).
- **Bright nights are a diagnosis, not always a decode error.** When the
  decoded light is non-zero through the night but keeps a coherent daily
  peak, the logger's own clock is wrong (the owner's HOBO2_PLES export
  shows 99,200 lux at "23:41" — noon sun): the file now decodes WITH a
  clock warning, and the pipeline's light-phase test gives the formal
  verdict. Only a glow with no daily cycle — the fingerprint of a wrong
  decode — is refused.

Blind validation (no export in sight, then compared): **124 files reproduce
their HOBOware export 100.0% exactly in temperature** (46 in v11.4.2), 120
also ≥ 99.9% in light. Calibration tables rebuilt from the 185 proven
alignments (462k pooled readings; light values carry HOBOware's own
rounding, whose last digit wobbles ±0.1 between rows of its own exports —
the sub-0.1% residuals are that wobble, not decode error).

## Drag-and-drop

Files can be dropped anywhere on the window: they land in **Data file(s)**
(renamed from "Data file") on the Qualification tab or **Database file(s)**
on the Visualization tab, running the same auto-detection as Browse.
(New optional dependency `tkinterdnd2`; the app runs without it.)

## Combined replicates: the spread column tells the truth

A row covered by a SINGLE replicate now carries an EMPTY spread instead of
0 — there was nothing to compare, and 0 read as "the replicates agreed
perfectly". The owner's alternating zeros came from replicates configured
at DIFFERENT sampling intervals (1 h vs 2 h); the combine step now logs a
warning when it detects that.
