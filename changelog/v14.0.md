# QCS v14.0 - auditable HOBO replicate decisions

> **MAJOR** release (v13.3.0 -> v14.0). Temperature qualification changes:
> reviewed exclusions are variable-specific and sustained unresolved replicate
> disagreement withholds the combined temperature pending review.

## Fixed

- **A reviewed temperature exclusion leaves light independent.** The versioned
  decision ledger applies to single files and combined products, with inclusive
  local-time bounds or the entire source file when bounds are empty. The eight
  historical all-variable exclusions are retained.
- **Closing replicate review does not accept its recommendation.** The operator
  must explicitly accept the advisory reference verdict. The matplotlib review
  uses the correct Button class and runs through the Qt interface-thread hook.
- **Combined reports describe the exported combined table.** Individual reports
  have stable source identities and a source map; prior unscoped reports remain
  recoverable. Batch refuses partial output after a failed replicate and disables
  both preference writers before booting its hidden shell. Error reporting gives
  the stopping error priority over earlier warning dialogs.

## New

- **Sustained unresolved disagreement withholds temperature.** Spread above
  0.5 degC for at least 24 elapsed hours and three consecutive eligible pairs
  produces an empty temperature with Flag_T=3. Agreement, missing pairs and
  gaps exceeding 1.5 median sampling steps break an episode. Isolated disagreements
  retain their suspect mean; one eligible contributor remains usable with empty
  replicate spread.
- **Replicate decisions and diagnostics are auditable.** Per-sample values,
  episodes, applied decisions and reference diagnoses accompany the product.
  Automatically bad or suspect readings remain available for diagnosis without
  becoming eligible temperature contributors. The ledger ships in the installer.

## Note

- **The initial 24-hour screen is a review policy, not proof of sensor failure.**
  Reference advice is never accepted automatically in batch. The recorded SGOM
  decision excludes HOBO1 temperature; it does not authorize exclusions at other
  sites. Corpus operations and input failures are recorded in
  `sourceCode/batch/CORPUS_LOG.md`.
- **HOBO times remain local GMT-03.** No timebase, light-window, Seaguard or
  Doppler qualification rule changes in this release. Existing qualified files
  need an explicit requalification to receive the new behavior.

## Install

Download and run **QCS_Setup_v14.0.exe**. Installing over an existing copy
upgrades it in place and preserves saved folders and interface preferences;
the new major version applies its version-gated QC defaults.
