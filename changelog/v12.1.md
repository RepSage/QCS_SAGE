# QCS v12.1 - polish round on the Qt shell

> **MINOR** release (v12.0 -> v12.1). The QC rules and the default criteria
> are UNCHANGED: no flag, threshold or test logic was touched.

## Fixed

- **File drag-and-drop.** Dropping onto a FIELD or a button did nothing - a
  QLineEdit/QTextEdit handles drops itself and would paste the path as text -
  so only drops on bare background worked, and the fields cover most of the
  window. File drops are now taken at the application level and routed to the
  active tab.
- **Go to visualization** landed on step 2 of whatever database was open
  before; it now returns to step 1 with the file just qualified.
- **Reset view** in the plot windows did nothing: the toolbar was customised
  through the tk API only, and under the Qt shell the whole block failed
  silently. Back, Forward, Pan and Save were checked at the same time.

## Data visualization

- The **regression degree** starts at 5 for every data type.
- The rarely-used variables carry their **Rarely used** heading again (it was
  lost in the port; they still start unchecked).
- The post-run **Open output folder** / **Go to visualization** buttons sit
  closer to RUN.

## Manual point cut, mooring data

The panel now **shades the manoeuvres**: the stretches where the depth series
says the instrument was being lowered or hauled up, with a dashed marker at the
water entry and at the start of the recovery. It is there to make the cut easy
to see - nothing is hidden or cut automatically.

The tide is never marked. Movement is measured as the NET vertical displacement
over two minutes: a tidal cycle moves centimetres per minute, a wave returns to
where it started, and only a real manoeuvre accumulates metres. Measured over
the whole mooring corpus (35 qualified files): a wave-exposed 6 s mooring went
from 84% of the record shaded under the first draft to 3.5%; the 10-min
moorings that are the routine here shade 0-4%.

## Selection summary

For a **Seaguard** selection the box now states the serial, the logging start,
the sampling interval and how many sensor groups the cast merges - all read
from the session folder names plus a bounded peek at the binary, so a 10 MB
session previews in 0.2 s instead of 1 s. The interval reported is the finest
group's, which is the axis the deployment is merged onto. A **CO2 data** row
names the added file, its readings and its own period, and repeats that its
clock is local.

## Installer

The finish page offers **Open the user manual** beside starting the program.
The folder page and the desktop-icon page keep their rule: shown on a fresh
install, skipped when an existing install is being upgraded.

## Verification

- Suite 52/52; ruff clean.
- Every interface change was measured on the running shell, and the depth
  shading on the archived corpus (35 mooring files, four cadences: 6 s, 1 min,
  10 min, 30 min).
