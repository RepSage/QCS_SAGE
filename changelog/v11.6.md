# QCS v11.6 — Drag-and-drop actually drops; the owner's polish round

> **MINOR** release (v11.5 → v11.6), from the owner's v11.5 field test.
> QC rules unchanged.

## Fixed

- **Drag-and-drop now works.** tkdnd drop targets do NOT propagate to child
  widgets, and v11.5 registered only the container frames - so dragging over
  the entries, labels and buttons that cover most of the window showed "no
  drop". Every widget in the tree is now registered individually (86 drop
  targets). Working drag-and-drop logs nothing — the Execution log is for
  pipeline messages, not infrastructure — and only its ABSENCE logs a
  diagnostic line.
- **The Light cutoff choice persists across sessions.** It silently fell
  back to "Reviewed (adaptive)" on every launch; it now saves and restores
  like the other preferences.

## New

- **The combined output folder carries `Temperature series.svg`** — the
  combined temperature at a glance, whatever the replicate count (the
  per-replicate light-window plots were already there).
- The combine method note (`QCS_combine_method.txt`) now explains the
  intentionally EMPTY `Flag` column of combined sheets: single-logger sheets
  carry their own per-test flag string there, a combined series has none
  (each replicate ran its own tests), and the column stays so both sheet
  kinds share one layout and stack cleanly into a database. The combined
  verdicts are `Flag_T` / `Flag_lux`.

## Answered, not changed

- The dashed green / dotted red lines in the light-window plot are CURVES
  by design since v10.0: they are the season-adjusted clean-sky baseline
  and fouling threshold (the legend says "season-adjusted"). Flat lines
  would misrepresent the rule actually applied; the curvature at the plot's
  extremities is the astronomical seasonal cycle of daylight.

## Verification

- Suite 52/52; ruff clean; 3/3 live-GUI checks (86 drop targets registered,
  light-mode round-trip); a real two-replicate raw-`.hobo` qualification
  shows `Temperature series.svg` in the combined folder.
