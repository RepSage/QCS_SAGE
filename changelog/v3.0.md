# QCS v3.0 — Revised qualification criteria

> **MAJOR** release (v2.2.1 → v3.0): the revised criteria change qualification
> results — the same raw file can be qualified differently than under the 2.x
> versions. Under semantic versioning, a change that breaks the comparability of
> results is exactly what justifies incrementing the major number.
>
> The criteria are grounded in QARTOD/IOOS + the standard Aanderaa SeaGuard II
> sensors + the oceanography of the Brazilian coast.

## Guiding principle
**Broad, general** criteria, valid for the **whole Brazilian coast** (from the South to
the Amazon reefs), with no regionalization and no seasonality. The design consequences:
- The **environmental ranges** act as a national climatological envelope (wide, so they
  catch only gross error), since they must accommodate everything from the Amazon plume
  (very low salinity) and upwelling/SACW (cold water) to oceanic Tropical Water.
- Fine control is left to the **relative tests** (spike, rate of change, flat line),
  which do not depend on region.

Methodological reference: the **QARTOD** framework (gross range, spike, rate of change
and flat line tests; flags 1/2/3/4/9), which QCS had in fact already adopted.

## 1. Sensor ranges (the instrument's limit — Aanderaa SeaGuard II)
These mark only what the sensor is physically able to measure. *(to be confirmed against
the exact model)*

| Parameter | Current | Proposed | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | -5 to 35 °C | **-5 to 40 °C** | typical range of the Aanderaa temperature/conductivity sensor |
| Salinity | 2 to 42 | **0 to 45** | derived salinity (4319) |
| Conductivity | 0 to 70 mS/cm | **0 to 75 mS/cm** | Conductivity Sensor 4319 |
| Pressure | 0 to 10050 dbar | **0 to 6000 dbar** | the line's maximum depth rating (body rated to 6000 m) |
| Oxygen | (does not exist) | **0 to 500 µM** *(deferred — a new test)* | Oxygen Optode 4831/4330; adding a sensor range changes the flag structure, so it goes together with the other new tests |

> Third-party sensors integrated through the analogue channel (Turner **Cyclops-7F** for
> turbidity/chlorophyll/CDOM and **AMT pH**) have a configuration-dependent range — keep
> a generous sensor range and check each one's datasheet.

## 2. Environmental ranges (climatological envelope — all of Brazil)

| Parameter | Current | Proposed | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 15 – 35 °C | **8 – 32 °C** | accommodates upwelling/SACW (cold, South/Southeast) and the equatorial surface (~30–31); below 8 is gross error only |
| Salinity | 34 – 38 | **20 – 37.5** | a minimum of 20 covers the Amazon plume's influence on the shelf (decided); it still catches gross freshwater contamination |
| Conductivity | 20 – 70 mS/cm | **5 – 75** (or disable) | depends on T and S; salinity is already the QC target — we suggest trusting salinity |
| Pressure | 10 – 300 dbar | **0 – 6000 dbar** | this is a deployment-depth check, not a property of the water; a minimum of 10 was failing the surface layer in profiles |
| pH | 7 – 8.5 | **7.5 – 8.4** | seawater is ~7.9–8.2; a minimum of 7.0 catches only gross failure |
| Chlorophyll | 0.01 – 4.5 µg/L | **0 – 30 µg/L** | minimum **0** (the current 0.01 was failing near-zero values, which are common and valid); a maximum of 30 accommodates coastal/upwelling/equatorial blooms |
| Oxygen | 190 – 400 µM | **120 – 450 µM** | the subsurface/SACW has low O₂; blooms supersaturate |
| Organic matter (CDOM) | 0 – 10 ppb | **0 – 50 ppb** *(to be calibrated)* | sensor/calibration dependent |
| Turbidity | 0 – 22 FTU | **0 – 50 FTU** *(to be calibrated)* | resuspension/river plume exceeds 22 easily |

## 3. Methodological changes to the tests

1. **Time-based flat line — DISCARDED.** The sampling frequency varies between
   moorings/profiles and, in profiles, "time" does not apply (the instrument descends
   through the column). The criterion by **sample count** (`rep_cnt_fail`/`rep_cnt_susp`)
   was kept, adjustable in Settings.
2. **Per-variable factors and window — DEFERRED** (likely v3.1). Today `fail_factor=3`,
   `susp_factor=2.5` and `time_window=30M` apply to every variable.
3. **Three-point spike test (QARTOD) — ADOPTED AND IMPLEMENTED.** Replaces the old method
   (iterative difference against the previous point) with `|V₂ − (V₁+V₃)/2|` in a single
   pass. It isolates the spike better, does not fail good data after regime changes
   (fronts) and is faster.

---

## 4. New tests

1. **Density inversion (profiles) — IMPLEMENTED.** The standard QARTOD test for CTD:
   potential density (`sigma0`, through `gsw`) may not decrease with depth beyond the
   tolerance. It **complements** the vertical gradient (both run). Tolerance
   **0.03 kg/m³**. It occupies **position 33** of the flag string (profiles) and
   implicates **temperature and salinity**.
2. **Attenuated signal / biofouling (long mooring)** — variance too low within a window
   indicates fouling on the optical sensors (chlorophyll/turbidity). *(future)*

> Note: the O₂ sensor range (a new test) remains **deferred**, to avoid asymmetry between
> the secondary sensors (decision: the environmental range already covers O₂).

## Decisions taken (June 2026)
- **Minimum environmental salinity = 20** (covers the Amazon plume on the shelf).
- **Three-point spike (QARTOD): adopted.**
- **Sensor ranges: the standard Aanderaa values were confirmed** (revisit if the exact
  model in use differs).
- **Chlorophyll: minimum 0** (lets near-zero values through).

## Open items (to be settled during implementation)
- A time criterion for the flat line (e.g. flat minutes) and per-variable factors.
- The tolerance of the density-inversion test.
- Handling of "≤ 0" for the optical variables (clamp to 0 vs flag).
