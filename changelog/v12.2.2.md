# QCS v12.2.2 - the visualization tab, three corrections

> **PATCH** release (v12.2.1 -> v12.2.2). The QC rules and the default criteria
> are UNCHANGED: no flag, threshold or test logic was touched.

## Data visualization

- **Recent says why it is greyed out**, as the qualification tab already did:
  "Clear the database file(s) to select a recent file". The box is usable only
  while nothing is selected above - either pick files or reopen a recent
  selection, never both at once.
- **Scale settings carries the "Rarely used" heading** in front of the same
  variables the parameter filter heads (Conductivity, Density, Soundspeed,
  Pressure on a Seaguard database). The tk step 2 headed both columns and the
  move to the new interface kept it only on the filter, so the two columns
  broke at different places.

## Interface

- **Every switch opens at the top of the page.** Changing tab, or stepping
  forward and back inside the visualization tab, used to land wherever that
  page had been scrolled to - stepping into the visualization tab could open
  it at the bottom. Measured: with both pages scrolled to their end, switching
  now arrives at 0, and so do Next and Back.
